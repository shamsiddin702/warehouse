# Stock Manager — warehouse system

An internal stock and location system for a shop. Not a shopfront: there is no
basket, no checkout and nothing for sale online. The boss uses it to answer one
question fast — **where is that product?** — plus what's running out and what's
going stale.

Plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript. No build step, no dependencies.

---

## Running it

Open `dashboard.html` in a browser, or serve the folder:

```powershell
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File serve.ps1 -Port 8777
# then visit http://localhost:8777
```

## Pages

| Page | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `dashboard.html` | Overview: counts, low-stock alerts, utilization per row, recent activity |
| `locations.html` | The 12 shop aisles + the back-room bulk bay, as warehouse cards |
| `products.html` | Full catalogue, searchable. **Click a row to reveal its shelf** |
| `stock.html` | Per-location stock records with min/reorder levels. **Click a row to reveal its shelf** |
| `transfers.html` | Moving stock between aisles and the back room |
| `reports.html` | Animated donut + line charts, utilization, alerts. Four tabs |
| `expiry.html` | Expired lines, expiring soon, and slow/dead stock |
| `lowstock.html` | Everything at/below minimum, grouped into an order sheet by supplier |

## Filtering stock by date

Stock Management has a **last restock date** filter with presets and a custom range:

- Restocked in the last 7 / 30 / 90 days
- Not restocked in 90+ days (things that have been sitting there)
- Custom from/to range

Presets are anchored to the demo's pinned "today" (26 Sep 2024), not the wall
clock, so the counts stay sensible whenever you run it. The table's Last Restock column
also shows a plain-English age ("12 days ago", "3 months ago").

## One filter per column

Stock Management has a **Filters** panel with a control for every column in the
table, so you can always see which column you're narrowing:

| Column | Control |
| --- | --- |
| Product | free text over name, brand, SKU, supplier and location |
| Location | dropdown of all rows plus the back room |
| Quantity on hand | min–max range |
| Min threshold | min–max range |
| Reorder qty | min–max range |
| Status | Any / Normal / Low stock / Out of stock |
| Last restock | presets or a custom from–to range |

Active filters appear as removable chips above the table, with a count badge on
the **Filters** button and a **Clear all filters** button. Pressing `/` opens the
panel and jumps to the product box. The All / Low / Out chips above the table are
a shortcut for the same Status filter, so they stay in sync with the dropdown.

## Location model

Every product sits at a **row** (aisle 1–12) and a **shelf** (1–5, top to bottom).
That pair is the answer the tool exists to give.

```
Row 10  ·  Shelf 2     Anti-Dandruff Shampoo 400ml
Back Room — Bulk Bay    39 units of overflow
```

**Locations are hidden until you click.** A collapsed row shows only the product,
its SKU, category and a location *count* — never the shelf. Clicking (or pressing
Enter/Space on a focused row) expands a panel with the exact row and shelf, a
shelf map of that row, stock levels, supplier, cost, margin and any back stock.
Clicking again collapses it.

## The expiry & dead stock page

Three reports, because "expired" and "not selling" are different problems:

- **Expired Products** — past their use-by date, with how many days ago and the
  cash value written off.
- **Expiring Within 3 Days** — plus how long the current stock takes to sell at
  its recent rate, so you can see whether it will actually clear in time.
- **Slow Moving & Dead Stock** — sorted by units sold in the last 30 days, with
  **days of cover** (how long the stock lasts at the current rate) and the cash
  tied up. Anything over 120 days of cover is worth discounting or writing down.

**Tuning the demo data.** Expiry and sales aren't random — they're driven by three
editable maps near the bottom of `assets/js/data.js`:

```js
const WATCH_AGED = { 'White Sliced Loaf 800g': 8, ... };  // days since delivery
const WATCH_SOON = { 'Coriander Bunch': 2, ... };
const WATCH_DEAD = ['Cat Scratching Post', ...];           // sold nothing in 30 days
```

Add or remove product names to change what shows up. Anything not listed falls
back to automatic ageing based on the product's own `sd` (shelf life in days).

## Data

`assets/js/data.js` holds everything:

- `LOCATIONS` — 12 aisles + back room, with zone, contact, capacity
- `CATEGORIES` — 12 departments with a colour each
- `PRODUCTS` — 203 real-ish grocery/household lines, generated by `p(...)`:
  `p(name, brand, cat, description, row, shelf, stock, capacity, reorder, cost, price, supplier, shelfLifeDays)`
- `STOCK` — one record per product, built from `PRODUCTS`; about a third also
  have back stock in the bulk bay
- `TRANSFERS`, `ACTIVITY`, `TREND` — history and the 7-day chart series

`TODAY` is pinned to 26 Sep 2024 so nothing drifts out of date as you demo.

## Consolidating spots (birlashtirish)

One product sitting in two crates? On **Stock Management**, any product with
records in more than one location gets a **merge** button (the branch icon) in
its row:

1. Pick which of its other locations to merge into — every unit moves there
   and the emptied record is removed, **freeing its spot**. The toast has an
   **Undo**.
2. Straight after, the app offers **Yangi mahsulot joylashtirish**: search the
   whole catalogue (type a name or SKU) and move any product into the freed
   row/shelf. Its own old spot becomes free in turn.

So "apples in two crates, one nearly empty" becomes: merge the low crate into
the full one, then drop a different product into the freed shelf.

## Inventarizatsiya (stock-take)

`inventory.html` compares the **system count** with the **counted (actual) count**:

- Type the actual quantity into the **Amalda** column — the difference and the
  status are computed live: **Mos** (match), **Kamomad** (shortage, red),
  **Ortiqcha** (surplus, blue).
- Each mismatching row offers a **→ N** button that sets the system stock to the
  counted figure (e.g. system says 50, you counted 47 → one click and it is 47).
  **Farqlarni tuzatish** corrects every mismatching line at once.
- **Saqlash** archives the count into the **Tarix** tab: every line keeps the
  *original* system figure, the counted figure, the difference, and whether it
  was corrected — a proper audit trail. Sessions can be expanded, exported to
  CSV, or deleted.
- Sessions persist in `localStorage` together with the rest of the edits.

## Importing products from Excel / CSV

**Products → Add Product → Choose file…** reads an `.xlsx`, `.xls` or `.csv`
right in the browser (SheetJS, bundled at `assets/js/vendor/` — nothing is
uploaded anywhere). It understands a 1C-style export:

| Column | Used as |
| --- | --- |
| Товар / Номенклатура (name) | product name |
| Остаток (stock) | units on hand (negative remainders are kept as-is) |
| Sotuv narx (sale price) | shelf price |
| Xarid narx (buy price) | cost price |
| Минимальный остаток, if present | minimum level |

Price cells like `27000 UZS` are **stored in so'm but shown as bare numbers**
(`27 000`) — no currency text appears on screen. Imported lines land in
**Row 13 — Electrical & Lighting** (category *Electrical & Lighting*, supplier
*File import*) and persist in `localStorage` like any other edit. Known brands
(Panasonic, Nilson, Gewiss, Viko…) are detected from the name prefix; the
header row and the qty/price columns are auto-detected, with a fallback to the
1C layout A / D / E / F.

## Everything is editable

There is no backend, so **every button works against `localStorage`** and the
changes survive a reload. Use **Reset demo data** at the bottom of the sidebar to
get back to the shipped figures.

| Where | Buttons |
| --- | --- |
| Products | Add Product, and per row: Duplicate, Edit, Delete (with Undo) |
| Stock Management | Add Stock Record, and per row: Record a delivery, Copy to another location, Edit, Delete |
| Warehouses | Add Warehouse, and per card: Edit, Delete |
| Transfers | New Transfer — actually moves the units and logs it |
| Reports | Download icon — exports a CSV |
| Expiry & Dead Stock | Export waste list — exports a CSV |
| Low Stock | Export order sheet — a purchase-order CSV grouped by supplier; per row: Record a delivery, Remove |

Every form validates (required fields, numbers, ranges, email format) and reports
errors inline. Deletes ask for confirmation and show what will be affected. Edits
re-render the page immediately, and the sidebar counts, dashboard alerts, charts
and expiry figures all follow.

New files:

- `assets/js/store.js` — the mutable state, `localStorage` persistence, and all
  the add/update/delete/transfer operations
- `assets/js/ui.js` — modal dialogs, toasts, confirm prompts, CSV export

### Adding a product

```js
p('Wholemeal Bread 800g', 'Mill Rise', 'bakery', 'Sliced, 28 slices',
  2, 1, 20, 40, 12, 0.85, 1.49, 'NF', 3),
```

`cat` must be a `CATEGORIES` id. The sidebar counts, filters, reports, charts and
utilization all pick it up automatically.

## Animation

Reports animate on load and replay when you switch tabs:

- stat numbers count up (with thousands separators)
- utilization and category bars grow from zero, staggered
- the donut sweeps its arcs in
- the line chart draws itself, then the dots pop in

All of it collapses to static under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Counters also have a
`setTimeout` fallback so they still reach their final value if the browser throttles
`requestAnimationFrame` in a background tab.

## Notes

- Search: `/` jumps to the search box from anywhere.
- Tables are keyboard-navigable — tab to a row, Enter or Space to expand.
- Filter buttons and tabs use `aria-pressed` / `aria-selected`.
- Modals trap focus, close on Escape or backdrop click, and submit on Enter.
- Everything is demo data: names, suppliers, contacts and figures are invented.
  Changes persist in your browser only — there is no server, and nothing is shared
  between browsers or machines.
